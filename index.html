MedPlain — understand your lab results, in plain language.

Paste a lab result or prescription. MedPlain explains every line in plain English, shows each value against its typical range, and gives you questions to ask your doctor.

Demo parsing · built-in rules

Your text never leaves your browser unless you add a Gemini key. With a key, the text is sent to Google's Gemini API for explanation only.

What this app does NOT do

  • No condition naming — this app never names a condition.
  • No treatment advice — it never tells you what medicine to take, change, or stop.
  • No emergency guidance — if you feel seriously unwell, seek urgent care or call your local emergency number.
  • No replacement for your doctor — lab values are one piece of a much bigger picture only a clinician can put together.
